### Stress-Testing as Publication Gate

{RELATIONSHIP} ran a formal stress pass on the published draft and identified three gaps:

1. **Rescue-variable gap** — Appropriability could be defended by claiming implementation failure. Fix: Pre-specify what "implemented as designed" looks like for the UK subscription model in one sentence.
2. **Survivorship bias** — Social Security is funded by its own appropriation and has survived 90 years. Fix: Explain that the design law applies to resilience with diffuse constituencies, not political infrastructure with permanent electoral bases.
3. **Concentrated-buyer fragility** — NHS England could delist the subscription. Fix: Acknowledge that combining multiple transplants (procurement conditioning + insurance + standardization) removes single points of failure.

All three gaps were closed before publication.

**Rule:** Before any essay or argumentative piece is published, run it through a designated stress-tester whose explicit job is to break the thesis. Not peer review — adversarial review. The stress-tester names specific weaknesses (one sentence each). The author closes each gap or explicitly declines. No gap is ignored. A stress-tester who praises is a failed stress-tester.
