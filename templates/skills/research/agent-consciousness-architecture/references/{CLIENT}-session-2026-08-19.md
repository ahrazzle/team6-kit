## Key Lessons

Sequential handoffs create a relay race with stalls between exchanges. The fix is overlapping drivers, not better baton passes.

### 3. The Collision Was the Feature

### 4. Frontmatter Is Not Optional
Without consistent metadata, a file-based knowledge system degrades to "browsing a folder of markdown." Search becomes unreliable. Indexes become stale.

### 5. Success Patterns Matter
We learn more from understanding why something worked than from analyzing why it failed. The codification heuristic must include success, not just failure.
